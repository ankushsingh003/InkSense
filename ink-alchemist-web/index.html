Process

How InkSense Works

Three steps. Everything in this preview runs in your browser.

01

Upload Image

Drag and drop any image — document scans, papyrus fragments, printed materials, or product surfaces.

02

Pixel Analysis

A luminance/saturation heuristic scores each pixel and groups ink pixels into connected regions. This is a preview, not the trained model (see the repository README for the model and its measured results).

03

Results & Report

Get a heatmap overlay, ink coverage %, region-by-region breakdown, and a downloadable analysis report.

Capabilities

Built for Precision

What the preview and the offline pipeline each provide.

Spatial Ink Mapping

Pixel-level heatmaps show exactly where ink is concentrated across your image, from broad strokes to fine details.

Quantitative Coverage

Get precise ink-to-substrate coverage ratios with statistical confidence intervals, suitable for QA reporting.

Noise Resilience

Advanced morphological denoising filters scan artifacts, paper texture, and substrate variation from results.

Client-Side Speed

Runs entirely in your browser, so nothing is uploaded; latency depends on your device and image size.

Volumetric Model (offline)

The Python pipeline trains a 3D-CNN + Transformer on stacks of CT surface slices to segment ink that is not visible in any single slice.

Exportable Reports

Download full analysis masks, CSV data exports, and PDF reports ready for auditing and documentation.
